# Personas

## Underwriter or business user

Needs answers grounded in approved documents and wants confidence that a
referral decision can be explained.

## Data scientist

Needs to discover permitted data, compare training runs, understand dataset
changes, and reproduce a selected model.

## AI or application engineer

Needs to build a RAG application, identify the source and index version behind
an answer, inspect traces, and diagnose retrieval or model failures.

## Governance and risk owner

Needs evidence of source approval, evaluation thresholds, model or application
release decisions, policy boundaries, and retained audit information.

## Platform administrator

Needs tenant isolation, secure connections, durable delivery, observability,
backup and recovery, upgrade safety, and clear component ownership.
